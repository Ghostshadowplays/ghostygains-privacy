Ghosty Gains Privacy Policy
Last updated: 26 October 2026

Ghosty Gains (“the App”) is developed by GhostyWare. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Information We Collect
Ghosty Gains does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All app data — including goals, timers, workout logs, preferences, and settings — is stored locally on your device and never leaves your phone.

2. No Account or Login Required
Ghosty Gains does not require you to create an account or sign in.
We do not access any external services or identity providers.

3. No Internet Communication
The App does not send any data to external servers.
It does not use analytics, tracking, or remote logging.

4. No Third‑Party Services
Ghosty Gains does not use:

Advertising networks

Analytics SDKs

Cloud storage

Social media integrations

Payment processors

5. Permissions
Ghosty Gains may request the following permissions:

Notifications — to send reminders and progress updates

Foreground service — to run timers and goals reliably

These permissions are used only for core app functionality and never for data collection.

6. Children’s Privacy
Ghosty Gains is intended for general audiences.
No personal data is collected from users of any age.

7. Changes to This Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page.
